Episode 20: Zoë Zebra the Postman's Daughter / Friendly Postman Mail

[We begin at Peppa's house as she announces the episode title. Mr. Zebra, the Postman, and his daughter, Zoë, are in his van, and with the toot of his horn, they appear at the house; he then posts a letter.]
Narrator: Mr. Zebra, the postman, is delivering a letter to Peppa's house.
[Zoë jumps out of the van and walks over to Peppa's front door, checking everyone's letters.]
Narrator: Zoë Zebra is with her daddy today. She has her own letters to deliver.
Zoë Zebra: Hmm. Danny Dog, Suzy Sheep, Rebecca Rabbit... Ah, Peppa and George. [posts the letter] Just these ones left.
Mr. Zebra: Good. Let's get them delivered.
Zoë Zebra: Yes, Daddy.
[They both jump into their van and drive off. Meanwhile, Peppa and George run down the stairs to the front door.]
Peppa Pig: Post!
[Peppa and George giggle. Peppa picks up the two envelopes and walks over to Mummy Pig.]
Narrator: Peppa and George love it when the post arrives.
Peppa Pig: We've got two letters.
Mummy Pig: This one's for Daddy Pig.
Daddy Pig: (snorts) How exciting! [opens the envelope and then reads the letter.] "Dear Mr. Pig, please pay your telephone bill." [smile fades] Oh.
Peppa Pig: What's a bill? It sounds very boring.
Daddy Pig: (chuckles) Bills are very boring.
Peppa Pig: Mummy, is the other letter for you?
Mummy Pig: No. This one's for Peppa and George.
Peppa Pig: Wow! [She opens the envelope, which uncovers a pink invitation letter.] (snorts) A letter for George and me. [She hands the invitation over to Mummy Pig.]
Mummy Pig: "Please come to my birthday party. Love, Zoë Zebra."
Narrator: Zoë Zebra has invited Peppa and George to her birthday party.
Peppa Pig: Yippee!
[Peppa giggles and George snorts twice.]
Mummy Pig: We need to write a reply to Zoë to tell her you're coming to her party.
Peppa Pig: Can you help us write it, Mummy?
Mummy Pig: Of course!
Narrator: Peppa and George are writing a reply to Zoë.
Mummy Pig: (snorts) What would you like to say?
Peppa Pig: Dear Zoë.
Mummy Pig: "Dear Zoë."
Peppa Pig: Umm... I don't know what to say next.
Mummy Pig: How about (writes) "We would love to come to your party. Kind regards, Peppa and George."
[Peppa and George giggle.]
Daddy Pig: (snorts) What kind of envelope shall we use?
Peppa Pig: [picks out the red envelope] The red one.
[Peppa hands the red envelope over to Mummy Pig. Mummy Pig places the letter inside the envelope, turns it over, and writes down Zoë's name on the back.]
Mummy Pig: "Miss Zoë Zebra."
Daddy Pig: And last of all, the stamp.
[Peppa giggles, takes the stamp from Daddy Pig's hand, and slams it on the top-right corner of the envelope.]
Narrator: The letter is ready to be posted.
